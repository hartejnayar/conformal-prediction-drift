# Research Questions

## Working title

Efficiency vs. Reliability: A Comparative Analysis of Conformal Prediction Methods Under Covariate and Concept Drift

## Primary research question

How do selected conformal prediction methods trade off empirical coverage, prediction efficiency, and computational cost under controlled distribution shifts?

## Candidate subquestions

1. How does empirical coverage change as the magnitude of distribution shift increases?
2. How do prediction-set size or interval width and computational cost change across methods?
3. How quickly do online methods recover after abrupt versus gradual changes in the data-generating process?

## Definitions

- **Covariate shift:** The input distribution changes while the conditional relationship between inputs and labels remains stable.
- **Concept drift:** The relationship between inputs and labels changes over time.
- **Coverage:** The fraction of outcomes contained in the reported prediction sets or intervals.
- **Efficiency:** How compact the prediction sets or intervals are, subject to their reliability.
- **Computational cost:** Runtime and, where feasible, memory usage.

## Research status

All questions are provisional. No research gap, method superiority, or experimental result has been established. Revise these questions after reviewing related work and discussing feasibility with a faculty mentor.
